## When to Invade

Invading is a chaos tool — use it when you want to disrupt the enemy jungler's rhythm rather than play an even farming game.

The cost of invading is that **both junglers fall behind** in gold and XP while chasing each other around. For a farm-heavy jungler with strong dueling but weak gank threat (like Graves), this is worth it when the enemy jungler is heavily gank-dependent or level-reliant (Amumu, Fiddlesticks). Graves wins the early 1v1 with his E dodge and Grit armor stacks. Without invade pressure, those junglers simply outscale him in team impact as the game progresses.

The flip side: if the enemy jungler doesn't adapt well to being invaded, they'll fall behind the XP/gold curve. Neither jungler will be level 4 in time for the first scuttle fight if they've been skirmishing — the one who came out ahead and adjusts back to farming first wins the mid game.

---

## Factors for Invading

**Take the flat HP shard** if you're planning to invade. The stat difference at level 1–2 is significant and can snowball a lead far more than a scaling HP shard ever would at that point in the game.

**Lane priority** is the key enabler. If your mid laner or side laner on the side you're invading has priority (they're ahead in lane or pushing), your opponent has to decide between peeling for their jungler or collecting their minion wave. If your laner is pushed in, they can't rotate — you're invading without a safety net.

## Common Invade Paths

### Level 1: Late buff contest

Show up to the enemy's buff camp a few seconds after it spawns at 0:55 to contest them at level 1. Both junglers are level 1, so it's a pure fighting check.

### Level 1: Raptor-side invade

Ward the Raptor bush or the enemy's Red buff before camps spawn. If they're on Red buff, you can cut to Krugs and meet them there. If not, take their Red and continue clearing your own camps.

### Level 2: Small camp invade

Take one camp on your side (typically Red or Raptors), then immediately cross over to their Gromp. Starting Raptors is a good option here — you can clear them without smiting, arriving at the invade with smite available while the enemy has likely already burned theirs on their buff. If you start Red instead, smite it — the buff duration plus the walk to Gromp takes too long without it.

- If they started Blue, they'll be low by the time they reach Gromp — easy to fight or steal
- If they started Red, you can play for both their Blue and Gromp

If you don't see the enemy when you cross over, they've started the opposite side. Drop a ward between their Blue buff and Wolves — it covers the main pathing corridor and tells you where they're headed so you can decide whether to push deeper or back off.

**Sample path:** Red → enemy Blue/Gromp → Raptors → Wolves → Gromp

## Invade Principles

### Prioritize small camps over buffs

When counterjungling, target small camps (Raptors, Wolves, Gromp) rather than buffs. Small camps respawn faster and level up quicker, so stealing them compounds over time. Buff camps take longer, give the enemy a buff when they recollect, and are less efficient to steal per second spent.

### Krugs are hard to counterjungle — leave them

Krugs take a long time to kill and are deep in the enemy's jungle. Invading Krugs risks getting collapsed on mid-clear. Leave them and target faster camps instead.

### Raptors are the most vulnerable to counterjungling

Because Raptors are quick to steal and respawn fast, **they are the most important camp to protect** if you think the enemy jungler will invade. Prioritize their respawn window — if you're not there when they come up, you're handing over an easy steal.

---

## Camp Scaling Follow-Up

When you invade and clear enemy camps, those camps respawn at a higher level — worth more gold and XP. If you don't play for those respawns, you've actively made their camps more valuable for them to collect.

The correct follow-up to a successful invade is to **contest their respawns** — deny them the scaled camps you created, rather than leaving the extra gold and XP on the table for the enemy jungler.
