# Rune Notes

General notes on rune selection — not champion-specific unless noted.

## Coup de Grace vs Cut Down

| Rune | Bonus | Condition |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Coup de Grace | +8% damage | Target below 40% HP |
| Cut Down | +8% damage | Target above 60% HP |

**They're mirror images of each other** — same bonus, opposite windows.

### When to take Coup de Grace

- **Jungler default.** You're usually arriving to ganks where laners have been trading — target is often already at 50–70% HP and will dip below 40% partway through your combo. Coup fires reliably.
- Against squishy targets (ADC, mid assassins) who melt quickly and spend most of a fight sub-40%.
- When your combo has high burst and you're likely to cross the 40% threshold mid-rotation.

### When to take Cut Down

- Against tanks or bruisers who stay above 60% HP for most of the fight (high base HP, lots of healing/shields).
- When you're not the primary damage dealer and fights drag out — Cut Down punishes high-HP targets throughout.
- Less reliable for a jungler since targets are rarely at full HP when you arrive.

### The nuance

A fight against a 3000 HP tank who sits at 80% HP for most of the trade is a Cut Down game. A gank on a 1800 HP ADC who's at 55% HP is a Coup de Grace game — they'll drop below 40% fast.

Neither rune is relevant against objectives or minions.
