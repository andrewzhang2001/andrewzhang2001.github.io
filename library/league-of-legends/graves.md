## Why Play Graves

- Exceptionally strong 1v1 fighter with high armor (True Grit stacks passively) and burst-range hybrid playstyle
- Dominates squishy, less-mobile AD junglers — out-damages them before they can react
- Ghostblade/Youmuu's active gives enough mobility to skirmish and disengage
- Falls off against AP-heavy or CC-heavy compositions where he can't reach his damage efficiently

---

## Matchup Tier List

*Source: Challenger tier list. General rule: wins into squishy AD threats without strong CC or high sustained AP damage. Loses into high-DPS AP mages, stat-check fighters (Yi, Udyr), and tanks that shrug off his burst.*

### Hardest

Karthus, Mundo, Lee Sin, Master Yi, Malphite, Diana, Lillia, Brand, Gwen, Udyr, Taliyah, Rumble

**Why:** High-DPS AP champions melt through his armor (which only mitigates physical). Stat-checkers like Yi and Udyr run him down post-6 or outscale him. Lee Sin can out-duel early. Rumble/Taliyah/Lillia are too slippery or out-damage him over time.

### Skill Matchups

Kha'Zix, Kindred, Nidalee, Jayce

**Why:** Outcome depends heavily on who plays the skirmish better. Nidalee and Kha'Zix can trade poke but Graves wins in a committed fight. Kindred is a race — invade before they hit marks. Jayce has range and poke but loses all-ins.

### Slightly Graves Favored

Hecarim, Jax, Ambessa, Naafiri, Nocturne, Pantheon, Trundle, Teemo, Volibear, Zha'an

**Why:** Graves generally wins or trades even in full-commit fights. Most of these are AD threats he out-damages or has enough armor to absorb. Don't get caught off guard by Nocturne R or Pantheon R ganks.

### Annoying (Teamcomp Problem, Not 1v1 Loss)

Amumu, Ivern, Fiddlesticks, Rammus, Cho'Gath, Poppy, Nunu, Zac, Shen

**Why:** Graves doesn't hard-lose 1v1 to any of these, but they provide so much team CC that he can't function in teamfights. Amumu/Zac/Shen are ult-reliant engage champs — Graves can invade and bully them before they hit spike. Rammus is rough to all-in due to Thornmail + passive.

### Easy

Briar, Rengar, Aatrox, Jarvan IV, Shaco, Rek'Sai, Elise, Kayn, Evelynn, Ekko, Fizz, Talon, Bel'Veth, Olaf, Camille, Maokai, Viego, Vi, Qiyana, Xin'Zhao, Shyvana, Zed, Wukong, Sejuani, Ornn, Sylas, Morgana, Warwick

**Why:** Squishy or less-mobile AD assassins/fighters that Graves out-armor-tanks and out-bursts. Tanks like Maokai/Sejuani/Ornn lose 1v1 but are annoying in teamfights — invade them before they build.

## General Gameplan

**Priority: gold lead above all else.** Graves is a scaling skirmisher — the more items he has relative to the enemy jungler, the harder every 1v1 becomes. A 2-item Graves beats most junglers who are 1-item.

- **Farm over everything.** Clear your camps on cooldown. Scuttle when safe. Do not abandon full clears for questionable ganks.
- **Trade objectives for camp leads.** If the enemy is taking Dragon and you have 3 camps up, take the camps. A camp lead is worth more than a contested objective you're likely to lose anyway.
- **Never take losing objective fights.** Don't show up to a Baron/Dragon where the enemy team is ahead in items or numbers. Stall, clear, and wait for a better window.
- **Plan around not dying.** Deaths are doubly punishing — you lose gold and fall behind on camps. If a fight is 50/50, disengage. Your job is to outfarm and outscale, then pick fights you win cleanly.

## Power Spikes

| Spike | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Serrated Dirk | First major lethality breakpoint — noticeably increases burst damage |
| First completed item | Full item passive comes online (Hubris Eminence, Collector execute, etc.) |
| Second Dirk | Lethality doubles up; squishy targets start dying to single combos |
| Second completed item | Two item passives active simultaneously; peak mid-game power |
| Last Whisper | Armor pen vs. targets starting to build armor — extends damage into mid game |
| Lord Dominik's | Full armor pen + Giant Slayer against tanky/high-HP targets; late-game damage spike |

## Combo Reference

### AA → E reload cancel

Immediately after an auto attack, you can input E before the reload completes. As long as the auto's pellets land **after** you start the E animation, the reload triggers instantly — effectively skipping the reload animation.

In practice: fire AA → input E the moment the shot leaves the gun → pellets hit during the E dash → gun is already reloaded when the dash ends. This lets you get back to full ammo faster than a normal reload and makes the `AA → E → AA` sequence significantly tighter than it looks.

**Key rule:** the pellets need to physically connect during the E animation — if you fire too early and the pellets land before you press E, the reload proceeds normally.
