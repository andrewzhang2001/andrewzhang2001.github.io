## Respawn Timers

| Camp type | Respawn |
|-----------|---------|
| Small camps (Raptors, Wolves, Krugs, Gromp, Scuttlecrab) | 2:15 |
| Buff camps (Blue, Red) | *To be filled in* |

You have a timer for your own camps automatically. Enemy camp timers only show if you had vision when the camp was cleared — so knowing the standard clear order lets you estimate respawns without vision.

### Key timers to memorize

**Red → Krugs**
Cleared around **1:35** → respawns **~3:50**

**Raptors**
Cleared around **1:15** → respawns **~3:30**

These two are worth internalizing because you can use them to plan your second clear and identify when to contest or invade the enemy's side. If you know they cleared Raptors at 1:15 and it's 3:25, their Raptors are about to come up — you can be there.

## Camp Scaling

Monster level on respawn is calculated from the **average champion level of both teams** at the time the camp spawns. This means camps scale up naturally as the game goes on — a Raptor camp cleared at 3:30 and recollected at 10:00 will be significantly stronger than it was at first spawn.

Epic monsters (Dragon, Baron, Herald) scale differently — they upgrade based on **game time**, not champion levels.

## Scouting Respawn Timers

If your team didn't see a camp get cleared, you can still get an accurate respawn timer by gaining vision of the camp's location within a window before it respawns:

| Camp type | Vision window needed |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Buff camps (Blue, Red) | 60 seconds before respawn |
| Small camps | 10 seconds before respawn |

If you gain sight outside that window, the timer won't register — you'll just see the camp is gone with no countdown.

## Jungle Pets

Pets evolve at **15 treats** and **35 treats**.

**Treat generation:**
- Killing a large monster gives 1 treat
- A bonus treat accumulates in storage every 60 seconds — this stored treat is consumed automatically the next time you kill a large monster, giving 2 treats for that kill

The bonus treat storage incentivizes steady, consistent clearing over frantic back-to-back farming. If you kill large monsters faster than every 60 seconds, you're leaving bonus treats unused.

## Camp Notes

### Respawn timer start

A camp's respawn timer does not begin until **every monster in the camp is dead**. If you kill the Greater Murk Wolf but leave a small Wolf alive, the timer doesn't start until the last Wolf dies.

If the large monster dies, any remaining small monsters are automatically killed after **10 seconds** of returning to a neutral state — so the timer eventually starts on its own, just with that delay. If you only clear the large monster and leave the smalls, expect your respawn to be ~10 seconds later than normal.
