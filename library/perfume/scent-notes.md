# Scent Notes
{: .no_toc }

First impressions and what I'm learning about what I like.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

---

## What I'm drawn to: aquatics

After trying a bunch of different scent families at Sephora (with help from a manager there), I kept coming back to aquatic fragrances. The way I'd describe it: they give you space while still being pleasant to smell. Refreshing but not overbearing. Some of the floral ones I tried felt like they suffocated my nose — aquatics don't do that.

---

## Favorites so far

### Dolce & Gabbana — Light Blue Pour Homme

The one that left the strongest impression at Sephora. Clean, fresh, immediately likable.

- **Type:** Citrus Aromatic
- **Top:** Grapefruit, Bergamot, Sicilian Mandarin, Juniper
- **Mid:** Pepper, Rosemary, Brazilian Rosewood
- **Base:** Musk, Incense, Oakmoss
- **Vibe:** Mediterranean freshness — bright citrus up front, herbal and woody underneath. A warm-weather classic.

### Clean Reserve — Rain

Another aquatic. Similar vibe — light and refreshing. The name kind of nails it.

- **Type:** Aromatic Aquatic (unisex)
- **Top:** Bergamot, Chilled Cucumber, Waterlily
- **Mid:** Dewy White Flowers, Cyclamen, Muguet
- **Base:** Patchouli, Rainforest Vetiver, Clean Musks
- **Vibe:** The dewy scent that lingers after a rainfall. Crisp, watery cucumber and airy musk with earthy vetiver and patchouli underneath.

### Frederic Malle — L'Eau D'Hiver

My date night fragrance. Same thread as the others — soft, not suffocating — but more refined and layered.

- **Type:** Floral Woody Musk (unisex)
- **Top:** Bergamot, Green Notes, Pink Pepper, Lemon, Grapefruit, Calamus
- **Mid:** Heliotrope, Iris, Hawthorn, Violet, Honey, Lily of the Valley, Jasmine, Orange Blossom, Rose, Ylang-Ylang
- **Base:** White Musk, Angelica, Hay, Cedar, Tonka, Sandalwood, Benzoin, Amber
- **Vibe:** Powdery and tender — a cloud of heliotrope and hawthorn with sweet almond and honey that feels weightless on the skin. Minimalist despite the long note list. Created by Jean-Claude Ellena.
