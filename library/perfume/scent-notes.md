# Scent Notes
{: .no_toc }

First impressions and what I'm learning about what I like.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

---

## What I'm drawn to: aquatics

After trying a bunch of different scent families at Sephora (with help from a manager there), I kept coming back to aquatic fragrances. The way I'd describe it: they give you space while still being pleasant to smell. Refreshing but not overbearing. Some of the floral ones I tried felt like they suffocated my nose — aquatics don't do that.

---

## Favorites so far

### Dolce & Gabbana — Light Blue Pour Homme

The one that left the strongest impression. Clean, fresh, immediately likable.

### Clean Reserve — Rain

Another aquatic. Similar vibe — light and refreshing. The name kind of nails it.

---

## Scent families I've tried

| Family | Impression |
|--------|-----------|
| **Aquatic** | My favorite so far. Refreshing, gives space, not suffocating |
| **Floral** | Too heavy for me. Felt overbearing and cloying |
